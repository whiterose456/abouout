A little about me

I like connecting ideas across disciplines.

My interests move between machine learning, AI agents, cybersecurity, and the engineering foundations that make software reliable. I’m especially interested in understanding systems deeply: how models learn, how applications fail, how protocols behave, and how separate components can work together.

Outside coursework, I use independent projects and technical reading to explore topics that catch my attention. I also enjoy chess, writing, and learning about fields beyond computer science. I’m still developing my expertise, and I value careful experimentation, clear documentation, and honest engineering over inflated claims.
