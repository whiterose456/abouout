02 / A little context

More interested
in the why.

I’m a computer science undergraduate at BINUS University in Indonesia. I like learning by taking systems apart, building small experiments, and following questions beyond the syllabus.

PAComputer science
undergraduateIndonesia · 2026
GitHub ↗ LinkedIn ↗

My interests move between machine learning, AI agents, cybersecurity, and the foundations of software engineering.

I’m drawn to questions that sit between fields: how models learn, how applications fail, how protocols behave, and how separate components can be orchestrated into useful systems. I use independent projects and technical reading to explore those questions, and I’m still working out where I can contribute most deeply.

Outside the technical side, I enjoy chess, writing, and learning about subjects beyond computer science. I value curiosity, clear explanations, and being honest about what I know—and what I still need to figure out.
